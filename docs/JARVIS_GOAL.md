## The daily experience

One continuing Main chat remains home. Talking should be as easy as sending a message; interruption, correction and stopping must work during real execution. Side chats provide optional focus. Files, tasks, settings and the computer open as separate pages without filling chat history with navigation. The original animated companion remains responsive to actual assistant state. A cinematic visual style must not make everyday actions harder.

Completed work should be immediately recognizable. New file-producing replies now show the deliverable first and compact source links underneath, rather than presenting every document read as a new result. Desktop and Android-emulator acceptance verifies direct source access, exact file bytes and draft preservation; it does not replace the separate live-model and voice acceptance gates.

Spoken replies now retain their recording as a compact row when its transcript exactly matches the visible reply. This keeps one readable conversation instead of duplicating each answer in another full card. Distinct recordings retain their transcript. Playback, download, recovery and navigation remain part of acceptance; reduced history clutter does not close the outstanding natural-response-speed gate.

The assistant handles ordinary preparation and reversible work directly within its enabled capabilities. It asks for a missing decision when that decision matters, and uses the existing exact reviews for consequential external actions. It reports actual work and uncertainty rather than narrating intentions as completed results. Model choice and connection management stay optional details after setup.

## How progress is judged

Voice recovery now has Android emulator and iPhone Simulator evidence. Native testing exposed a timing error where large capture callbacks could turn a brief noise into a submitted utterance. The original capture path now evaluates at most 20 ms at a time; a 240-case rate/buffer/offset matrix and the native journeys pass. Both noise recovery and failed-speech retry reuse the completed reply without repeating assistant work. Two earlier fresh iPhone simulators stalled before app launch; the later diagnostic device completed migration and passed the current lifecycle journey. The earlier failures remain recorded, and the cause of their different startup behavior is not established. This advances dependable conversation without closing physical-audio, response-speed or overall product gates.

Saved clarifications can now continue through an ordinary chat or voice answer, retaining the original task and using the existing owner message and run. A separate conversational topic leaves the question pending. A dedicated internal decision now binds the answer or chooses a new request before replying or doing work. A simple unrelated answer can finish in that decision; no new owner step is added. One automatic recovery is available when the model skips the decision. Repeated unsupported text fails explicitly. This reduces the need to switch to a separate answer field, but interpretation still depends on the model. The complete actual-model Mac journey now verifies a topic change, an exact typed-answer email review and a spoken filename answer that produces one exact file and preserves the typed draft. Its 78.2-second speech-end-to-audio time remains too slow for everyday conversation; synthetic audio does not close physical-acoustics testing. See [conversational continuation](FOLLOW_UPS.md) and the retained live-model failures in the verification record.

Local Quick requests now share the compact capability catalog used by voice. Explicit plain-text requests also require a fingerprinted, host-bound saved result before claiming completion; other capabilities remain discoverable. The latest full 27B Mac continuation journey (`compact-quick/live-27b-decision`) passes all five checks, including recovery from an actual fabricated file-completion claim. Its spoken continuation takes 62.1 seconds from utterance end to audio with synthetic input. That is functional progress, not a general speed claim. Smaller-model trials still confuse a separate topic with a pending answer, and one earlier trial created an unsolicited file. The failed receipts remain part of acceptance; model reliability and conversational concision remain open. A broader typed capability check then found an invented skill save. Required answers now remain on their device through page navigation and full app termination, independently of the main message draft. Native iPhone and Android acceptance covers typing a missing email recipient, restoring a partial answer, continuing once, and retaining one exact unsent review.
